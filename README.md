<h1 align="center">Roomy</h1>
<p align="center"><b>Room for what matters.</b> See what fills your Windows drive and safely free up space.</p>
<p align="center"><a href="https://pithiwat.github.io/roomy/">Website</a> · <a href="https://github.com/pithiwat/roomy/releases/latest">Download</a></p>

- **Space map** — your whole drive as boxes; find what takes up space in seconds
- **Safe cleanup** — Windows, browser, graphics, app and game caches; always asks before deleting
- **Move folders to another drive** — programs still find them; move them back anytime *(Pro)*
- **Duplicates and old files**, **Docker/WSL disk compaction** *(Pro)*, **automatic maintenance** *(Pro)*
- Thai and English · Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) · free download, 7-day Pro trial
