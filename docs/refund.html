14-day money-back guarantee

If Roomy Pro is not right for you, you can get a full refund within 14 days of purchase. No questions asked.

How to ask

Find your receipt email from Paddle and use the link in it, or go to paddle.net and look up your order. Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com. Paddle.com is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Paddle provides all customer service inquiries and handles returns.

After a refund

The license key from that order stops working in the next Roomy update, and Roomy goes back to the free version. Folders you moved keep working and can always be moved back.
