Refund Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

Refunds through Microsoft

Roomy Pro is sold through the Microsoft Store, so refunds are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Store refund policy. We support refund requests made within 14 days of purchase.

How to ask

Sign in at account.microsoft.com → Order history, find Roomy Pro and choose Request a refund. To stop a subscription renewing, cancel it at account.microsoft.com/services.

After a refund

Roomy goes back to the free version the next time it checks your purchase with the Microsoft Store. Folders you moved keep working and can always be moved back.

Try first

Every Pro feature can be tried free for 7 days in the app before you buy, with no card needed.
