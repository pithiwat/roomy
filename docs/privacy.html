Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 November 2026

In short

Roomy works on your PC. It does not collect, upload or sell information about your files, and it has no analytics or tracking.

The app

Scans, cleanups and folder moves happen only on your computer. Settings, license status, move history, memory and disk history and the activity log are stored on your PC. The app connects to the internet only to: check your Roomy Pro purchase with the Microsoft Store (Microsoft Store version); check for a new Roomy version on GitHub (version installed from our website); and, when you use the connection check or software updater, ping public servers (1.1.1.1, 8.8.8.8) and ask the Windows Package Manager sources for new versions. These services can see your IP address like any website.

Purchases

Payments are processed by Microsoft through the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not receive your payment details. Microsoft may share sales reports with us (for example country, product and date) as described in its terms.

License keys

A license key we issue directly contains the name and email it was issued to, so the app can show who it is licensed to. It is stored only on your PC.

Website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts; both may log technical data such as your IP address. The site sets no cookies of its own; your language choice is kept in your browser.

Your rights

You can ask us what data we hold about you and ask us to correct or delete it: github.com/pithiwat/roomy/issues (do not post personal data publicly; ask us for a private channel).

ตัวโปรแกรม
