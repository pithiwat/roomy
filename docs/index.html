Questions

Is it safe? Will it delete something important?

Roomy only deletes caches and temporary files that Windows and programs recreate on their own. It skips files in use, never touches documents, photos or work files, and asks before every cleanup. Anything you choose to delete yourself goes to the Recycle Bin, so you can restore it.

What happens after the 7-day trial?

Roomy goes back to the free version on its own. You're never charged, and no card is needed in the first place. Folders you moved keep working and can always be moved back.

Can I cancel monthly or annual?

Yes, any time, from the link in your receipt email. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for, then Roomy goes back to the free version. Nothing breaks and moved folders keep working.

Does freeing memory or sleep mode break anything?

No. Freeing memory doesn't close any app; Windows gives each app back what it needs. Sleep mode only stops programs from starting by themselves. Nothing is uninstalled, and you can wake them up any time.

How do I use it after buying?

Right after payment, your license key appears on screen. Open Roomy, press the Pro button at the bottom left, and paste the key. Done.

Can I move to a new PC?

Yes. Remove the license from the old PC on the Pro screen, then enter the same key on the new one.

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Roomy is new, so Windows SmartScreen may warn you. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer comes straight from Roomy's GitHub page.

Which Windows versions?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. Install it just for you or for everyone on the PC.
